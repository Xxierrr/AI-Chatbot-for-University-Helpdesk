Q: What is the full name of the university and where is it located?
A: The university is National Technical University (NTU), located at Knowledge City, Tech Zone IV, Innovation Valley Road, State of Innovation - 560100.

Q: What accreditation and rankings does NTU hold?
A: NTU is accredited with NAAC Grade 'A++' (institutional score 3.82/4.00), holds NBA Tier-1 accreditation for all undergraduate engineering programs, is approved by AICTE and UGC, and is ranked among the Top 20 Technical Universities in NIRF.

Q: What are the eligibility criteria for B.Tech admissions?
A: Candidates must have passed 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects along with Chemistry/Biotechnology/Computer Science/Biology with at least 60% aggregate (55% for SC/ST/PwD) and be at least 17 years old by December 31.

Q: Which entrance exams are accepted for B.Tech admission at NTU?
A: 60% of B.Tech seats are filled through JEE Main ranks via centralized counseling, and 40% of seats are filled through the NTU Engineering Entrance Examination (NTUEE).

Q: How can I apply for undergraduate admissions at NTU?
A: You can register online at the admissions portal (https://admissions.ntu.ac.in), fill out the application form, upload required academic and category documents, pay the application fee of Rs. 1,200 (Rs. 600 for SC/ST/PwD), and submit your branch preferences.

Q: What is the admission process for M.Tech programs?
A: Candidates holding a relevant B.E./B.Tech degree with at least 60% marks (or 6.5 CGPA) are admitted directly based on valid GATE scores. Non-GATE candidates must appear for the NTU Postgraduate Entrance Test (NTUPGET) followed by a technical interview.

Q: What are the entrance exams accepted for MBA admission at NTU?
A: NTU accepts CAT, MAT, XAT, CMAT, and the university's own NTU Management Aptitude Test (NTUMAT), followed by Group Discussion and Personal Interview.

Q: What is the selection process and fellowship for PhD programs?
A: PhD admission requires a Master's degree with minimum 55% marks and qualifying the NTU Research Aptitude Test (RAT) or holding UGC-NET/CSIR-NET/GATE, followed by a research proposal presentation. Selected full-time scholars receive an NTU Institute Research Fellowship of Rs. 35,000 to Rs. 40,000 per month.

Q: What is the annual tuition fee for B.Tech programs?
A: The total annual academic fee for B.Tech across all branches is Rs. 1,65,000 (comprising Tuition Fee Rs. 1,25,000, Lab Fee Rs. 15,000, Development Fee Rs. 12,000, Library Fee Rs. 5,000, Activity/Sports Fee Rs. 4,000, and Exam Fee Rs. 4,000).

Q: What are the one-time charges payable at the time of admission?
A: The one-time fee is Rs. 20,000, which includes a non-refundable Admission Processing Fee (Rs. 5,000), refundable Institute Caution Deposit (Rs. 10,000), refundable Library Deposit (Rs. 3,000), and Lifetime Alumni Membership (Rs. 2,000).

Q: What are the semester fee payment deadlines?
A: Odd semester fees must be paid on or before July 25, and Even semester fees must be paid on or before January 5. A 10-day grace period is provided without penalty.

Q: What is the late fine for delayed fee payment?
A: After the 10-day grace period, a late fine of Rs. 100 per day is charged up to 30 days. Beyond 30 days, registration is temporarily suspended and a re-registration fee of Rs. 5,000 applies.

Q: What payment modes are accepted for tuition and hostel fees?
A: Fees must be paid online via the NTU Fee Portal using Net Banking, UPI, Debit/Credit cards, or via NEFT/RTGS and Demand Draft in favor of "Registrar, National Technical University". Cash payments are not accepted.

Q: What is the university's fee refund policy if a student withdraws admission?
A: 100% refund (minus Rs. 1,000 processing fee) is given if requested 15 days or more before classes start; 90% if less than 15 days before classes; 80% within 15 days after classes start; 50% between 16 and 30 days; and no refund (except refundable security deposits) after 30 days.

Q: Which undergraduate engineering branches are offered at NTU?
A: NTU offers B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering (240 seats), Electronics & Communication Engineering (180 seats), Mechanical Engineering (120 seats), Civil Engineering (90 seats), Electrical Engineering (120 seats), and Information Technology (120 seats).

Q: How many academic credits are required to complete a B.Tech degree?
A: Under the Choice-Based Credit System (CBCS), students must complete 160 credits over 8 semesters, including core courses, professional electives, open electives, mandatory internships, and a major capstone project.

Q: Is internship mandatory for undergraduate engineering students?
A: Yes, an 8-week summer internship at the end of the 6th semester in industry or reputed research labs (such as ISRO, DRDO, CSIR) is mandatory and carries academic credits.

Q: What is the weightage of internal and external assessments in theory courses?
A: Theory courses are assessed via 40% Continuous Internal Assessment (Mid-Term Exam 20%, Quizzes 10%, Assignments 5%, Attendance 5%) and 60% End Semester Examination.

Q: How does the 10-point grading system work at NTU?
A: Letter grades correspond to: O (10.0, 90%+), A+ (9.0, 80-89%), A (8.0, 70-79%), B+ (7.0, 60-69%), B (6.0, 50-59%), C (5.0, 45-49%), P (4.0, 40-44%), and F (0.0, Fail below 40%).

Q: What is the minimum passing criterion for a course?
A: A student must obtain at least 40% marks in the End Semester Examination and at least 40% overall (CIA + ESE combined) to pass a course.

Q: What is the minimum CGPA required to graduate with a B.Tech degree?
A: A minimum Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 5.00 is required for B.Tech, and 5.50 is required for M.Tech, MBA, and MCA programs.

Q: What is the minimum attendance requirement to write end-semester exams?
A: A minimum of 75% attendance in each registered theory and laboratory course is mandatory. Condonation up to 65% is permissible by the Dean of Academics solely on valid medical grounds or university sports/cultural duty.

Q: What happens if a student has less than 65% attendance?
A: The student is marked "Debarred/Detained (DE)" in that course, is not allowed to sit for the end-semester exam, and must re-register for the course in a subsequent semester.

Q: How can I apply for revaluation of exam papers?
A: Students can apply for answer script inspection (Rs. 300 per subject within 10 days of results) or challenge evaluation/revaluation (Rs. 1,000 per subject within 15 days of results) through the Examination Portal.

Q: When are supplementary/backlog exams conducted?
A: Supplementary examinations are conducted annually in July during the summer break for students with 'F' grades.

Q: What are the conditions for Academic Probation?
A: A student who earns an SGPA below 4.00 in two consecutive semesters is placed on Academic Probation, given a faculty mentor, and restricted from extra credit registration or student club leadership.

Q: Are hostel accommodations available for all students?
A: NTU provides on-campus residential accommodation for over 4,500 students in 5 Boys' Hostels (BH-1 to BH-5) and 3 Girls' Hostels (GH-1 to GH-3) with single, double, and triple sharing options.

Q: What are the annual hostel room charges?
A: Annual hostel fees are: Triple Non-AC Rs. 36,000; Double Non-AC Rs. 48,000; Double AC Rs. 70,000; Single Non-AC (final year & PG/PhD) Rs. 60,000; and Single AC (PG/PhD) Rs. 85,000, plus Rs. 6,000 annual maintenance.

Q: What are the mess charges and what meals are included?
A: The annual mess charge is Rs. 42,000 (Rs. 21,000 per semester), which includes breakfast, lunch, evening snacks/tea, and dinner under FSSAI-compliant quality standards.

Q: What are the hostel curfew and in-time rules?
A: The entry curfew is 09:30 PM from Sunday to Thursday, and 10:30 PM on Fridays, Saturdays, and nights before holidays.

Q: How do students apply for an overnight leave pass from the hostel?
A: Students must submit an Outstation Leave Pass request on the NTU Student Portal at least 24 hours in advance, which requires automated SMS/OTP confirmation from their registered parent or guardian.

Q: Who is the Chief Warden and how do I contact the hostel administration?
A: The Chief Warden is Prof. (Dr.) Sudhir K. Sharma (email: chiefwarden@ntu.ac.in). The 24x7 hostel caretaker helpdesk can be reached at +91-80-2999-1122.

Q: What are the Central Library opening and closing hours?
A: Regular hours are Monday - Friday 08:00 AM - 11:00 PM; Saturdays & Sundays 09:00 AM - 08:00 PM; and during examination periods hours extend from 08:00 AM to 02:00 AM.

Q: How many books can an undergraduate student borrow from the library?
A: Undergraduate students can borrow up to 5 books for a period of 14 days, with one renewal allowed if the book is not reserved by another user.

Q: What is the library overdue fine policy?
A: An overdue fine of Rs. 2 per book per day is charged for the first 7 days, and Rs. 5 per book per day thereafter.

Q: Which electronic databases and journals are accessible via the library?
A: Subscriptions include IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, ASME, ASCE, Elsevier ScienceDirect, SpringerLink, JSTOR, Scopus, Web of Science, and EBSCO Business Source Ultimate, accessible on and off-campus via MyLOFT/Shibboleth.

Q: Does the library offer group study rooms?
A: Yes, the Central Library features 6 soundproof Group Discussion Pods that can be booked online 48 hours in advance for 2-hour sessions.

Q: What merit scholarships are offered by NTU?
A: NTU offers the Chancellor's Gold Scholarship (100% tuition waiver for JEE Main rank < 2,000 or NTUEE Top 5) and the Vice-Chancellor's Merit Scholarship (50% tuition waiver for 95%+ in 10+2 or top 1% department rank).

Q: What need-based financial aid is available at NTU?
A: Merit-cum-Means financial aid offers 25% to 75% tuition fee waivers to meritorious students whose annual family income is below Rs. 3,00,000. Applications close on September 15.

Q: Which government scholarships can NTU students apply for?
A: Students can apply for National Scholarship Portal (NSP) schemes, AICTE Pragati (for girls), AICTE Saksham (for PwD), PMSSS (for J&K/Ladakh), and respective State Post-Matric Welfare scholarships.

Q: Whom should I contact for scholarship queries?

Q: What was the highest and average placement package in 2023-2024?
A: The highest international package was Rs. 62.50 LPA (Microsoft Redmond), the highest domestic package was Rs. 54.00 LPA (Atlassian India), the overall average package was Rs. 11.80 LPA, and the CSE/IT average package was Rs. 16.40 LPA.

Q: What was the overall placement rate for graduating students?
A: The placement rate was 94.2% across eligible B.Tech and MCA candidates, with 1,420 total offers from more than 280 companies.

Q: Which top companies recruit from NTU campus?
A: Recruiters include Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Adobe, Atlassian, Oracle, Cisco, McKinsey, BCG, Deloitte, Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, Larsen & Toubro, Tata Motors, Bosch, and Texas Instruments.

Q: When does campus placement preparation begin?
A: Structured placement training begins in the 2nd year with DSA bootcamps, followed in the 3rd year by full-stack workshops, aptitude training, and soft skills coaching, and mock interviews by alumni in the final year.

Q: What medical facilities are available on campus?
A: NTU has a 20-bed Health Center with full-time doctors, 24x7 emergency medical care, 2 ACLS ambulances, and cashless tie-ups with Apollo Hospitals and Fortis Healthcare (covered up to Rs. 2,00,000 under student health insurance).

Q: What sports and fitness facilities are available at NTU?
A: The campus has an 8-lane 400m synthetic athletic track, football stadium, cricket ground with turf nets, floodlit tennis and basketball courts, volleyball courts, a 6-court indoor badminton hall, table tennis lounge, and a 5,000 sq. ft. modern gym.

Q: What food and cafeteria options exist on campus?
A: Facilities include the Central Food Court (Subway, Nescafe, Dosa Plaza, Chaayos, Amul), departmental tea/snack kiosks, and a Night Canteen operating from 10:00 PM to 03:00 AM.

Q: Is there an on-campus bank and post office?
A: Yes, there is a full-fledged State Bank of India (SBI) branch with 3 ATMs, a PNB ATM, and a Sub-Post Office offering Speed Post and parcel services on campus.

Q: How does campus public transportation work?
A: Free electric eco-shuttles circulate the campus every 10 minutes from 07:30 AM to 10:00 PM. A fleet of 15 university buses provides optional daily pickup and drop across 12 city routes for day scholars at Rs. 24,000 per year.
