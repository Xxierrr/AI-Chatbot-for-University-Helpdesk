2. ADMISSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
National Technical University maintains a transparent, merit-based admission policy compliant with state and national statutory guidelines.

2.1 Undergraduate Admissions (B.Tech Programs)
Eligibility Criteria:
- Candidates must have completed 10+2 (Higher Secondary) or equivalent examination from a recognized central/state board (CBSE, ISC, State Boards).
- Compulsory subjects: Physics and Mathematics, along with one optional subject among Chemistry, Biotechnology, Computer Science, Biology, or Technical Vocational subjects.
- Minimum Aggregate Marks: 60% aggregate in Physics, Mathematics, and the chosen optional subject (55% for SC/ST/PwD candidates).
- Minimum Age: Candidates must have attained 17 years of age as of December 31 of the admission year.

Accepted Entrance Examinations:
1. National Joint Entrance Examination (JEE Main) - 60% of total seats are allocated via Centralized Counseling based on All India Rank (AIR).
2. NTU Engineering Entrance Examination (NTUEE) - 40% of total seats are filled through the university's institutional entrance test conducted in computer-based mode.

2.2 Postgraduate Admissions
A. Master of Technology (M.Tech):
- Eligibility: Bachelor's degree in Engineering/Technology (B.E./B.Tech) in relevant disciplines with a minimum of 60% aggregate marks or 6.5 CGPA on a 10-point scale.
- Selection: Direct admission for candidates with valid GATE scores. Non-GATE candidates must appear for the NTU Postgraduate Entrance Test (NTUPGET) followed by a technical interview.
- Sponsored Candidates: Minimum 2 years of full-time industrial/research experience required; entrance test waived, selection via interview.

B. Master of Business Administration (MBA):
- Eligibility: Bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% aggregate (45% for reserved categories).
- Accepted Scores: CAT, MAT, XAT, CMAT, or NTU Management Aptitude Test (NTUMAT).
- Selection: Composite score of Entrance Test (50%), Group Discussion (20%), Personal Interview (20%), and Academic Record/Work Experience (10%).

C. Master of Computer Applications (MCA):
- Eligibility: BCA / B.Sc (Computer Science/IT) / B.Sc / B.Com / B.A. with Mathematics at 10+2 level or graduation level, with at least 50% aggregate marks.
- Selection: NTU MCA Entrance Test (NTU-CET) merit rank.

2.3 Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) Admissions
- Offerings: Engineering, Computer Applications, Management, Applied Sciences, and Humanities.
- Eligibility: Master's degree in relevant discipline with minimum 55% marks or 6.0 CGPA (50% for SC/ST/PwD).
- Selection Process:
  1. NTU Research Aptitude Test (RAT) consisting of Research Methodology (50%) and Subject Knowledge (50%).
  2. Exemption from RAT: Candidates qualifying UGC-NET, CSIR-NET, GATE, CEED, or holding national research fellowships (INSPIRE, JRF).
  3. Presentation of Research Proposal and Personal Interview before the Departmental Research Committee (DRC).
- Fellowship: NTU Institute Research Fellowship of Rs. 35,000/month for the first two years, upgraded to Rs. 40,000/month for the third and fourth years subject to satisfactory research progress.

2.4 Step-by-Step Application Process
Step 1: Online Portal Registration - Visit https://admissions.ntu.ac.in and create an applicant account with email and mobile number.
Step 2: Profile & Academic Information - Fill in personal details, educational qualifications, entrance exam roll numbers, and category details.
Step 3: Upload Scanned Documents - Upload recent passport photograph, signature, 10th and 12th marksheets, caste/category certificate (if applicable), and entrance exam scorecards.
Step 4: Application Fee Payment - Non-refundable fee of Rs. 1,200 (Rs. 600 for SC/ST/PwD) paid via Credit Card/Debit Card/UPI/Net Banking.
Step 5: Choice Filling & Locking - Select preferred engineering disciplines and program choices in order of preference.
Step 6: Seat Allocation & Document Verification - Monitor seat allotment results, download provisional allotment letter, and attend in-person document verification.
Step 7: Tuition Fee Payment & Enrollment - Pay the initial admission/semester fee to confirm enrollment and receive the Student ID/Registration Number.

5. EXAMINATIONS & EVALUATION SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The University operates on a Semester Pattern with Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) and End Semester Examinations (ESE).

5.1 Evaluation Weightage
A. Theory Courses:
- Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA): 40%
  * Mid-Term Examination: 20%
  * Quizzes / Surprise Tests (Best 2 of 3): 10%
  * Assignments, Case Studies & Term Papers: 5%
  * Class Attendance & Active Participation: 5%
- End Semester Examination (ESE): 60%

B. Practical / Laboratory Courses:
- Continuous Lab Performance & Viva-Voce: 40%
- Laboratory Records & Project Work: 20%
- End Semester Practical Exam & External Viva: 40%

C. Major Capstone Project:
- Mid-term Progress Review: 30%
- Project Report & Code/Prototype Demonstration: 40%
- Final Oral Defense & External Viva: 30%

5.2 Grading System (10-Point CGPA Scale)
NTU follows an absolute and relative grading framework mapped to a 10-point scale:

Grade   Grade Points   Percentage Equivalent   Performance Qualitative Description
O       10.0           90% and above           Outstanding
A+      9.0            80% to 89%              Excellent
A       8.0            70% to 79%              Very Good
B+      7.0            60% to 69%              Good
B       6.0            50% to 59%              Above Average
C       5.0            45% to 49%              Average
P       4.0            40% to 44%              Pass
F       0.0            Below 40%               Fail
Ab      0.0            Absent                  Incomplete
W       -              -                       Withdrawn

Calculations:
- Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) = Sum(Credits_i * GradePoint_i) / Sum(Credits_i) for all courses in that semester.
- Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) = Sum(Credits_all * GradePoint_all) / Total_Earned_Credits across all completed semesters.

5.3 Minimum Passing Criteria & Academic Progression
- To pass a course, a student must secure at least 40% marks in the End Semester Examination AND at least 40% in aggregate (CIA + ESE).
- Minimum CGPA required for award of B.Tech Degree: 5.00
- Minimum CGPA required for award of M.Tech/MBA/MCA Degree: 5.50
- First Class with Distinction: CGPA >= 8.50 with no backlogs throughout the program.
- First Class: CGPA >= 6.75 and < 8.50.
- Second Class: CGPA >= 5.00 and < 6.75.

5.4 Attendance Requirement
- Minimum 75% attendance in each registered theory and lab course is mandatory to be eligible to appear for the End Semester Examination.
- Condonation: Up to 10% relaxation (65% to 74%) may be granted by the Dean of Academics on genuine medical grounds (accompanied by valid hospital admission proof) or official representation of the university in sports/cultural events.
- Students with less than 65% attendance are marked "Debarred/Detained (DE)" in that subject and must repeat the course in a subsequent semester.

5.5 Revaluation and Script Verification
- Students dissatisfied with their End Semester evaluation may apply for:
  1. Photocopy / Digital Inspection of Answer Script: Rs. 300 per subject (within 10 days of result declaration).
  2. Challenge Evaluation / Revaluation: Rs. 1,000 per subject (within 15 days of result declaration). If the revaluation yields a change of 10% or more marks, 50% of the revaluation fee is refunded.

5.6 Supplementary Examinations & Backlog Clearance
- Supplementary exams are conducted once every academic year during the summer break (July) for students with F grades.
- Students can carry a maximum of 4 active backlogs from the 1st and 2nd years to be promoted to the 3rd year (5th semester).
- Maximum duration to complete B.Tech (4 years): 6 consecutive academic years.

5.7 Academic Probation
- A student securing an SGPA below 4.00 in two consecutive semesters is placed on Academic Probation.
- Students on probation are assigned a dedicated faculty mentor, restricted from taking overload credits, and barred from holding office in student clubs.
